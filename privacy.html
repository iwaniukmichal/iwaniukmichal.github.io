Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

motion-studio uploader (“the app”) is a personal tool, run by Michal Iwaniuk, that uploads videos he made to accounts he owns on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos, thumbnails and captions, and to read and update the signed-in channel’s videos and settings.
  • TikTok: the signed-in account’s basic profile (to confirm it is the right account) and permission to publish videos.
  • Instagram: the signed-in professional account’s basic profile and permission to publish Reels.

How the data is used

The app uses this access only to publish the owner’s videos and to check that each upload goes to the intended account. It does not read, collect or analyse other people’s data, show ads, or build profiles.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner’s computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platform the video is posted to. No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the account’s security settings (Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok and Instagram: their “Apps and websites” settings). Deleting the local token file removes the stored sign-in.

Use of Google data

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.
